Why Families Choose Us

Brickell International Academy — The Difference

Discover why families across Miami trust us with their children's education.

👩‍🏫

Expert Faculty

Our experienced faculty is dedicated to providing a high-quality education to our students. We have a low student-to-teacher ratio, ensuring that each student receives the individual attention and support they need to succeed.

📚

Small Classes

Our small class sizes allow our students to engage in hands-on learning activities and receive personalized attention from our experienced teachers.

🚫

Safe Campus

Our campus is a safe and welcoming environment for our students, with a secure entrance and exit, and a surveillance system in place to ensure the well-being of our students and staff.

📊

Curriculum

Our curriculum is designed to meet the needs of our students, with a focus on academic excellence, character development, and community engagement. Our experienced teachers use a variety of teaching methods to engage students and make learning fun.

👫

Whole-Child

We believe in educating the whole child, not just their academic skills. Our programs are designed to help students develop their social, emotional, and physical skills, as well as their academic abilities.

🌎

Community

We believe in the importance of community and social responsibility. Our school is committed to making a positive impact on the lives of our students and the community, through volunteer work, charity events, and environmental sustainability initiatives.

Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum that challenges, inspires, and prepares every student.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-Kindergarten program is designed for children aged 4-5, where they learn through play and exploration. Our experienced teachers provide a nurturing environment that fosters social, emotional, and cognitive development.

📚

Elementary

Our Elementary program serves students from Kindergarten to 5th grade, where they learn essential skills in reading, writing, mathematics, science, and social studies. Our experienced teachers use a variety of teaching methods to engage students and make learning fun.

📈

Middle/Upper

Our Middle/Upper program serves students from 6th to 8th grade, where they learn advanced skills in language arts, mathematics, science, and social studies. Our experienced teachers provide guidance and support to help students succeed in their academic pursuits.
