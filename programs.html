Academics & Enrichment

Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum designed to challenge, inspire, and prepare every student for success.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-Kindergarten program is designed for children aged 3-4, providing a nurturing environment that fosters social, emotional, and cognitive development. Our experienced teachers use play-based learning to engage students and prepare them for the transition to elementary school.

📚

Elementary

In our Elementary program, students in grades K-5 engage in a rigorous curriculum that combines academic excellence with creativity and critical thinking. Our small class sizes and experienced teachers ensure that every student receives the individualized attention they need to succeed.

📝

Middle/Upper

Our Middle and Upper School programs serve students in grades 6-8, offering a challenging and engaging curriculum that prepares them for high school and beyond. Our teachers use a range of teaching strategies to engage students and promote academic excellence.

💻

STEM

At Brickell International Academy, we believe that STEM education is essential for success in the 21st century. Our STEM program offers students a range of courses and activities that develop their skills in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics.

🎨

Arts

Our Arts program offers students a range of courses and activities that foster creativity, self-expression, and appreciation for the arts. From music and dance to visual arts and drama, our students have countless opportunities to explore their passions and develop their artistic talents.

🏆

Athletics

At Brickell International Academy, we believe that sports and physical activity are essential for a healthy and balanced lifestyle. Our Athletics program offers students a range of sports teams and activities that promote teamwork, discipline, and physical fitness.
