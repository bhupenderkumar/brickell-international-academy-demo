Academics & Enrichment

Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum designed to challenge, inspire, and prepare every student for success.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-Kindergarten program is designed for children aged 3-4, providing a nurturing environment that fosters social, emotional, and cognitive development through play-based learning and hands-on activities.

📚

Elementary

In our Elementary program, students in grades K-5 explore a rich curriculum that combines academic rigor with creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills, laying the foundation for future success.

📚

Middle/Upper

Our Middle and Upper School programs serve students in grades 6-8, offering a challenging and engaging curriculum that prepares them for high school and beyond, while also nurturing their emotional, social, and physical growth.

💻

STEM

At Brickell International Academy, we recognize the importance of STEM education in today's rapidly changing world. Our STEM program offers students in grades K-8 hands-on experiences in science, technology, engineering, and math, inspiring the next generation of innovators and leaders.

🎭

Arts

Our Arts program at Brickell International Academy provides students with a wide range of creative outlets, from music and dance to visual arts and drama, fostering self-expression, imagination, and a love of the arts that will stay with them throughout their lives.

🏈

Athletics

We believe that sports and physical activity are essential components of a well-rounded education. Our Athletics program at Brickell International Academy offers students a range of competitive and recreational sports, promoting teamwork, discipline, and a healthy lifestyle.
