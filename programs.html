Academics & Enrichment

Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum designed to challenge, inspire, and prepare every student for success.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-Kindergarten program is designed for children aged 3-4 years old. We offer a play-based curriculum that focuses on social, emotional, and cognitive development, as well as basic math and reading skills.

📚

Elementary

Our Elementary program serves students from Kindergarten to 5th Grade. We use a rigorous, inquiry-based approach to teaching that encourages critical thinking, creativity, and collaboration.

🎓

Middle/Upper

Our Middle and Upper School programs serve students from 6th to 8th Grade. We offer a challenging, project-based curriculum that prepares students for high school and beyond.

💻

STEM

Our STEM program offers students a hands-on, project-based introduction to science, technology, engineering, and math concepts. We use real-world examples and interdisciplinary approaches to make learning engaging and relevant.

🎨

Arts

Our Arts program offers students a range of creative outlets, from visual arts to music, drama, and dance. We believe that arts education is essential for developing imagination, self-expression, and confidence.

🏈

Athletics

Our Athletics program offers students the opportunity to participate in a range of sports, from team sports like soccer and basketball to individual sports like tennis and track and field. We emphasize teamwork, sportsmanship, and physical fitness.
