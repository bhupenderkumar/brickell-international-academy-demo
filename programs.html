Academics & Enrichment

Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum designed to challenge, inspire, and prepare every student for success.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-Kindergarten program is designed for children aged 3-4, where they engage in play-based learning activities that promote social, emotional, and cognitive development. Our experienced teachers provide a nurturing environment that fosters a love of learning and prepares students for success in elementary school.

📚

Elementary

Our Elementary program serves students in grades K-5, where they engage in a rigorous curriculum that emphasizes literacy, mathematics, science, and social studies. Our experienced teachers use a variety of teaching methods to meet the diverse needs of our students.

📖

Middle/Upper

Our Middle and Upper school programs serve students in grades 6-8, where they engage in a challenging curriculum that prepares them for high school and beyond. Our experienced teachers provide individualized support and guidance to help students achieve their academic goals.

🤖

STEM

Our STEM program is designed to inspire students to pursue careers in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. Our experienced teachers use hands-on activities and real-world applications to make complex concepts accessible and engaging for all learners.

🎨

Arts

Our Arts program offers students the opportunity to explore their creativity through music, visual arts, drama, and dance. Our experienced teachers provide individualized instruction and guidance to help students develop their artistic skills and express themselves confidently.

🏀

Athletics

Our Athletics program offers students the opportunity to develop their physical skills and teamwork abilities through sports such as soccer, basketball, and flag football. Our experienced coaches provide individualized instruction and guidance to help students achieve their athletic goals.
