Our Mission & History

Brickell International Academy was founded in 2010 with a vision to provide a high-quality education to students in the heart of Miami. Our school has grown steadily over the years, with a strong focus on academic excellence, character development, and community engagement.

Our teaching approach is centered around project-based learning, where students work collaboratively to solve real-world problems. Our experienced faculty uses innovative methods to engage students and make learning fun and interactive.

We offer a wide range of extracurricular activities, including sports, music, and art programs, to help students develop their passions and interests outside the classroom.
